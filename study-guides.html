Study guides

The Library holds short explainers, free to download. These are the longer ones: whole topics taken from the first idea to the last, written the same way and meant to be worked through rather than skimmed.

🚧 Work in progress. Nothing here is finished yet, and nothing is for sale. This page exists so you can see what is being written and how far along it is — not to take your money before the work is done. 📚

When a guide is ready it will be priced to be affordable for a student, because a guide a student cannot afford has failed at the only thing it was for. Everything already in the Library stays free. ✍️
