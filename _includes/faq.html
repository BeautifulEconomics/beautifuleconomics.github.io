What is Beautiful Economics?

A place to learn economics properly, free, with no account and no catch. Short entries explain one idea at a time; longer pieces take an argument apart. It is written for anyone curious — a school student, an undergraduate, a professor reading outside their field, or someone who just wants to understand the news.

Do I need to know any economics already?

No. Every concept entry starts from nothing and defines its terms. If a piece assumes something, it says so and links to the entry that explains it.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

It is free, and there is no catch. Nothing is sold here, there is no advertising, and no account is needed to read anything. The only thing ever asked for is an email address, and only if you want new pieces sent to you.

How is this different from a textbook or a YouTube explainer?

Each concept says three things: what the idea claims, what it quietly assumes, and where it stops working. Most explanations give you only the first. The third is the one that turns a definition into understanding — and the one most sources leave out.

Can I use this for my exams?

You can, and students do, but it is not exam coaching. Nothing here is written to a syllabus and there are no model answers. It is written to make the ideas make sense, which usually helps more than memorising, but check your own syllabus for what is required.

How often is something new published?

New concepts and essays are added as they are finished rather than to a fixed timetable. The quickest way to know is the email list — nothing else is sent to that address.

Can I quote or share this?

Yes. Link to it, send it to a student, quote it in an essay. Every page has its own permanent address, so a link you share today still works years from now. Please credit Beautiful Economics and link back.
