1. 01

    Pick an idea

    Open Concepts and choose anything that looks interesting. There is no order to follow and no prior knowledge assumed.

  2. 02

    See where it breaks

    Every entry ends with where the idea stops working. That is the part most explanations skip, and the part that turns a definition into real understanding.

  3. 03

    Keep what you learn

    Every page keeps its own permanent address, so you can come back, link it, or send it to someone. Nothing disappears into a feed.
