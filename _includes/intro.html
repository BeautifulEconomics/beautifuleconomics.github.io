{%- comment -%} The opening curtain. Homepage only, at most once every four hours. Everything is built by this script, so if JavaScript is off, slow or broken, no curtain is ever created and the homepage shows immediately. A timer removes it after 2.3s no matter what else happens, so the site can never be left sitting behind a blank screen. The drawing is one of six, picked at random each visit. They are drawn here as SVG rather than loaded as images, so nothing is fetched from anyone else's server and nothing can arrive late or not at all. {%- endcomment -%}
